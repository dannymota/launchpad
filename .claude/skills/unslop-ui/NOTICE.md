- Source: https://github.com/heydamir/unslop

Changes made: the workflow, tells, tokens and checklist were adapted and condensed into new wording; the workflow was rebuilt as 25 steps (look, fix, prove) around seven surfaces (web app, dashboard, mobile, landing, form, settings, agent tool); tells were given ids and detection patterns; numbers were split into App, Marketing and Mobile columns and merged with values from the MIT sources below and from public before/after posts; conflicts between sources were set as tunable per-surface defaults; colors, radii and control sizes were remapped to a DaisyUI v5 custom theme; grade criteria, pre-work, data states and stress tests, an independent review, proof format, premium moves, and measure and redline scripts were added. unslop's story-video recipe is summarised in one paragraph; its images and scripts were not copied.
